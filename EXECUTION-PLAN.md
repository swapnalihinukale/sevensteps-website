**Business measurement and improvement**

Track the real progression: received enquiry → qualified/unsuitable/unknown/pending → consultation where used → quote → confirmed booking. Preserve direct-to-quote journeys. A booking must meet the photographer's actual confirmation condition; a button tap, WhatsApp open or submitted browser event is not a booking or proof of receipt.

Start with a simple register: anonymous enquiry identifier, received date, source if known, service/date/location interest, qualification/outcome, first response time, quote date/value and booking confirmation/value where supplied. Keep private client details out of public project documents and analytics payloads. Record lost/declined reasons where volunteered rather than guessing them.

Capture a baseline before launch when information is available. Choose the review interval from enquiry volume and the normal booking decision period; no arbitrary deadline or statistically unsupported uplift claim. Compare cohorts with equivalent time to mature. Separate changing traffic/source mix, seasonality and capacity from the effect of a website change.

Prioritise the next improvement from evidence: poor fit suggests clearer scope; slow replies suggest response operations; unanswered trust questions suggest stronger complete work/process proof; quote drop-off requires learning why; low relevant discovery may warrant accurate local visibility and useful service/place content. Consider collected revenue minus directly attributable fulfilment costs and owner time when those data are available. Do not optimise enquiry count at the expense of worthwhile work.
