# Your details for the Seven Steps website

Review the placeholder site first: its layout, story order, mobile navigation and enquiry section. Note what feels wrong or unclear. You can send your photos and business details after that review.

## After your review

| Send or confirm | Include |
|---|---|
| Your name and role | Exact brand name, your role, any team members you want shown and who approves the site |
| Two complete assignments | Original photos you permit us to publish, their order, public names/title, occasion, location and credits. Include a portrait for the about section if you want one. |
| Permission | Confirm image and client-name permissions. For a testimonial, provide the original wording and permission to use it. Keep private correspondence in your own storage. |
| Work you want more of | A few recent assignments you enjoyed, why they suited you, services you offer and work you decline |
| Your client process | How you prepare clients, what you deliver, booking/deposit terms and promises you can meet |
| Your enquiry address and replies | Your receiving email/number, public profile links, who replies, backup cover and a realistic response window |
| Domain and accounts | Exact domain spelling, registrar, current hosting/email setup and who controls each account. Share access through invitations, not passwords in this document. |
| Budget and limits | Build/ongoing budget, travel area, capacity, any deadline, language or accessibility needs |
| Privacy and upkeep | Who updates content, handles deletion requests and approves retention periods and provider data handling |

Leave prices or optional sections out if you do not want to publish them. We will keep missing facts marked instead of inventing them. Confirm your domain and receiving address before publication. You can review the site before setting those up.

## Help us understand your enquiries

If you have recent records, share a small summary using the [measurement sheet](MEASUREMENT.md): source, service/event month/area, human response time, suitability, quote and confirmed booking outcome. Include a reason only if the client supplied it or you can verify it.

Remove names, contact details and private messages. Keep unknown or pending outcomes as they are. You can quote without a consultation, and an unrecorded outcome is not a lost booking.
