# Owner input sheet

Prepared for a future conversation; not sent. The current user is not expected to know these answers. Independent public research and non-code concept work can continue meanwhile.

| Input | Useful answer or evidence | Decision it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and responsibility | Exact trading name, Yogesh's role, current team, who approves content and who receives enquiries | Accurate representation and a responsible contact |
| Desired work | Five recent jobs they want more of and examples they would avoid, with reasons | Audience, service emphasis and portfolio edit |
| Recent enquiries | An anonymised summary of 15–20 enquiries: source, service/date, response, quote, result and reason if known | Locate the actual business problem; retain unknown/pending outcomes |
| Economics and capacity | Real package/quote approach, direct costs and time, available dates, travel area, seasonal limits | Commercial fit, scope and whether more demand helps |
| Client experience | Who decides, why clients choose/decline, guidance before/on the day, deposits and delivery promises | Honest proposition, practical content and enquiry flow |
| Portfolio and permission | Original files for representative complete projects and films, credits, naming/location accuracy, publication consent, verifiable reviews | Select work and proof that may actually be published |
| Business ownership/access | Ownership and current status of both domain spellings, relevant registrar/hosting/email accounts, social profiles and eligible Business Profile | Later domain and account plan; do not put passwords in this document |
| Operations | Preferred enquiry channel and recipient, responder, expected response time, how bookings/content/analytics will be maintained | Delivery checks, handoff and sustainable scope |
| Project constraints | Budget for build and recurring services, any real deadline, brand assets, languages, accessibility or legal requirements | Feasible implementation choices |

Capture only the information needed for decisions. Aggregate the enquiry sample and omit clients' private messages or personal contact details. Credentials, when needed later, belong in the appropriate secure access process.

For the recent-enquiry sample, use one anonymised row per enquiry:

| Anonymous ID | Received date / source | Service / event month / area | First response time | Qualified / unsuitable / unknown / pending | Consultation if used | Quote date / value if supplied | Booking confirmed / lost / pending | Volunteered reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep direct-to-quote journeys visible. A booking uses the owner's actual confirmation condition. Do not treat unrecorded outcomes as losses.

Resolve identity, publishing rights, business claims, recipient and running costs before the affected work is published or connected to live accounts. A missing price does not prevent a visual concept, and a missing domain does not prevent a later local build. Mark the exact dependent task rather than treating the whole project as blocked.
