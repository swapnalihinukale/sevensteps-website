About the Artist

I am Yogesh
Repale

A passionate visual storyteller based in Maharashtra, I believe every photograph is a frozen moment of emotion, raw, real, and timeless. With over 7 years behind the lens, I've had the honor of capturing love stories, life milestones, and brand identities that speak volumes without a single word.
